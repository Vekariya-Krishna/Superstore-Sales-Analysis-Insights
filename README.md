# Superstore Sales Performance & Customer Analytics

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes Superstore sales data using **Power BI** to understand overall business performance, sales trends, profitability, shipping preferences, product categories, and payment behavior.

The dashboard is designed not only to display numbers but also to turn those numbers into a clear **business story**. The second page onward focuses on storytelling by explaining what is happening in the business, why it matters, and where improvement opportunities exist.

## 2. Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to answer the following key business questions:

* How is the business performing overall?
* Which product categories generate the most sales?
* Which shipping method do customers prefer?
* How does profit change over time?
* Which payment methods are most commonly used?
* Which regions show strong sales performance?
* Where are the opportunities for improvement and growth?

## 3. Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks the following core KPIs:

| KPI                   |  Value |
| --------------------- | -----: |
| **Sales**             |  $341K |
| **Profit**            |   $27K |
| **Orders**            |  5,000 |
| **Average Ship Days** | 4 Days |

These KPIs provide a quick overview of the business before moving into detailed analysis.

## 4. Storytelling Approach

Instead of presenting the dashboard as a collection of charts, the report follows a **business storytelling approach**. Each page focuses on a specific business question and explains the insights, their importance, and potential improvement opportunities.

### 4.1 Business Performance Overview

The business generated **$341K in sales** and **$27K in profit** from **5,000 orders**.

The average delivery time is **4 days**, providing an overview of both financial performance and operational efficiency.

The **Region slicer** can be used to explore regional performance and identify areas that are performing strongly or requiring improvement.

### 4.2 Shipping Performance Story

Shipping analysis shows that **Standard Class** is the most-used shipping mode.

This indicates that customers primarily prefer a reliable and affordable delivery option.

The analysis also highlights an opportunity to improve **First Class** and **Same Day** shipping options for customers who prioritize faster delivery.

### 4.3 Sales by Category Story

The category analysis shows that **Office Supplies** is the strongest-selling category.

**Furniture** follows Office Supplies, while **Technology** has comparatively lower sales in the analyzed view.

This creates a potential growth opportunity for Technology products. Cross-selling Technology products with Office Supplies could help increase sales and improve overall profitability.

### 4.4 Monthly Profit Story

Monthly profit analysis shows that profitability changes significantly across different months.

**October** records the highest profit at approximately **$18K**, highlighting a particularly strong period of business performance.

Understanding high-profit months can help management plan inventory, promotions, and sales activities more effectively.

### 4.5 Payment Mode Story

Payment behavior shows that **Cash on Delivery (COD)** is the most-used payment method, accounting for approximately **43% of sales**.

Online and card payments represent additional opportunities for digital payment adoption.

The business could encourage digital payments through small offers or incentives, potentially improving the adoption of online and card-based transactions.

### 4.6 Regional Performance

Regional analysis allows users to compare performance across the following regions:

* Central
* East
* South
* West

The overall business summary indicates that **Central and West** show strong sales performance, creating opportunities for further growth and expansion.

## 5. Key Business Insights

The analysis highlights several important findings:

* **Office Supplies** is the leading sales category.
* **Standard Class** is the most-used shipping method.
* **COD** is the dominant payment method.
* **October** shows the strongest monthly profit.
* **Central and West** demonstrate strong sales performance.
* **Technology** represents a potential growth opportunity.
* **Digital payments** have room for further adoption.

## 6. Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions can be considered:

### 6.1 Strengthen High-Performing Categories

Continue focusing on **Office Supplies** while identifying ways to increase profitability.

### 6.2 Grow Technology Sales

Use cross-selling and promotional strategies to increase **Technology** product sales.

### 6.3 Optimize Shipping Options

Maintain the strong performance of **Standard Class** while improving faster shipping services such as First Class and Same Day.

### 6.4 Increase Digital Payment Adoption

Introduce small incentives to encourage customers to use **Online and Card** payment methods.

### 6.5 Use Seasonal Insights for Planning

Analyze high-profit months and align inventory, promotions, and sales strategies accordingly.

### 6.6 Focus on Strong Regions

Study **Central and West** to understand the factors behind their strong performance and identify opportunities to replicate successful strategies in other regions.
